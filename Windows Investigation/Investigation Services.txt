List All Services

Get-Service
Displays all services on the local system.

Get-Service ServiceName
Shows details for a specific service by name.

Detailed Service Information

Get-CimInstance -Class Win32_Service | Format-List Name, Caption, Description, PathName
Provides detailed information about all services, including their display name, description, and executable path.

Stop-Service -Name Suspicious
Get-Service Suspicious | Stop-Process

Remove-Item C:\Windows\suspicious_file_path

SOC Use Case: Monitoring Windows services helps detect unauthorized or suspicious services that may indicate malware persistence or system tampering. Reviewing the executable path can reveal malicious binaries masquerading as legitimate services.

Remove-CimInstance : Get-CimInstance -ClassName Win32_Service -Filter "Name='suspicious'"
